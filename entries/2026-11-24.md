a. Look at the hospital bed while the doctor talks about the PICC line. It seems unfathomable that only a few months ago I spent three weeks in a bed just like that, for twenty-three hours a day. Marvel at the human ability to adapt to the most difficult of situations.

b. In the corner of my eye, I can see the cheery, chatty doctor start pushing the line in in sweeping motions like he’s playing the violin. He says it can only go two ways: up, or down. (I imagine this like a Y-junction.) We figure it’s gone up towards my neck by the gurgling sound I can hear somewhere inside my neck (a bizarre feeling) as he flushes the line with saline. He pulls it backwards, sits me up, and then tries again.

c. ECG stickers remind me of the night in hospital when I went tachycardic. I remember calling the nurse and lying there in my underpants as they attached a mess of cables to seemingly everywhere.

d. Find the whole day weirdly emotional.
