c. I like Joseph Goldstein’s distinction between love and attachment. He says if you look closely at the experience of love, it is an outward feeling. It’s generous, selfless, and unconditional. Attachment is the opposite. It always looks inward, with you at the centre. Knowing the difference makes it easier to let go, because love has no strings.
